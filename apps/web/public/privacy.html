Dragonpunchedle

Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Dragonpunchedle is a free fan game. It stores as little as it can: enough to keep your board, your streak and your place on the leaderboards. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Playing as a guest

Nothing is stored about you on our server. Your guesses for today are saved in your own browser (local storage) so a refresh doesn't lose them.

If you make an account

  • Your username. It appears publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Your password, if you sign up with one, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. Nobody, including us, can read it back.
  • Sign in with Google: we receive your Google account id and your first name, which suggests your username. We never ask for your email address, and we don't keep the access Google grants: it's used once to learn who you are, then thrown away.
  • Your guesses and when you made them, for your board, streaks and leaderboards.

Cookies

  • dp_session keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. Logging out deletes it.
  • dp_oauth exists only while you sign in with Google, for up to 10 minutes, to protect that sign-in from forgery.

Both are strictly necessary for logging in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Server logs

Like any website, the server records each request (address, time, IP address) in its logs, which are used to fix problems and to limit repeated login attempts. They are not used to identify or profile players.

Where it's kept

The game runs on Render and its database on Neon, both in the United States. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your account

Email pedromakesstuff0@gmail.com with your username. Once we've confirmed the account is yours, your account, guesses and sessions are deleted for good.
